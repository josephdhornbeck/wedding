A few things to know

&
When should I RSVP by?

The sooner the better — it makes the caterer very happy. You'll find your invitation code on your paper invitation; enter it on the RSVP page to reply for your whole household.

Are kids invited?

We love your little ones — truly. But to keep our day intimate (and the guest list from tripling), we're only able to include the children whose names appear on your invitation. If your invitation doesn't list them, please take the evening as a well-earned night off.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Plus-ones are by invitation. If yours includes one, your RSVP will show a spot to add your guest's name — if it doesn't, we've kept your invitation to named guests. Thank you for understanding!

What's the schedule?

Ceremony at 4:00 PM, a cocktail hour to follow (minus the cocktails), and the reception at 6:00 PM. The full hour-by-hour timeline lives on the Home page.
